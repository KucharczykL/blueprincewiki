The puzzle introduces the concept of performing mathematical operations without signs, or more precisely the signs are expressed by a color:
- blue = addition
- yellow = subtraction
- pink = multiplication
- purple = division

The concept is used in:
- [[Billiard]]
- [[45 Rooms Note]]
- [[Mathematics Worksheet]]
