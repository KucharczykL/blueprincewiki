# First Era (Ajera)
- [[Oris]] (real name unknown) ascended as king, unified the surrounding provinces, expanding his vast kingdom
- most records gone, only trade agreements and sales receipts exist
# Second Era
- the fourth king split domain into three, giving each to one of his children:
	- eldest - middle region - Orindia? - became [[Fenn Aries]] at the end of fifth era
	- middle child, daughter - southern region of  [[Eraja]]
	- youngest, northern land of [[Arch Aries]]
# Third Era
- sibling monarchs cooperating, their regions developing into unique cultures
- trade and transportation flourished
# Fourth Era
- [[King Desilets III]] took over, bad monarch, did something that shocked everyone from nobility to commoner
- he probably moved the capital but left the castle and court behind
- this planted seeds of revolt/revolution in city of [[Fenn]]
# Fifth Era (Era of Ruin)
- Desilets III died and [[Prince Leerson of Fenn]] proclaimed himself the true monarch
- civil war ensued
- Fenn was suppressed ("Pacification of Fenn") in a brutal manner and [[War of the Fifth Era]] started that lasted 22 years, and many people died, including Prince Leerson
- Arch Aries and Nuance joined cause with Leerson
- Oris had fallen, black flags replaced with red flags
- royal family exiled south
# Sixth Era (current)
- today there are three realms: [[Fenn Aries]], [[Arch Aries]], [[Eraja]]
