Once drafted/entered, your items will be frozen (cannot be used and don't increase).
You can unfreeze them by entering [[Sauna]] or [[Furnace]] but if [[Furnace]] is so close to the freezer that the temperature increase, you will no longer be able to re-freeze them. [^1]

You can also melt the ice with [[Burning Glass]] (the items in the chest freezer must be defrozen this way), and you can break the wall of ice with [[Power Hammer]].
