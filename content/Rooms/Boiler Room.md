Needs ducts to lead energy to other rooms. Rooms with ducts:
- [[Weight Room]]
- [[Passageway]]
- [[Darkroom]]
- [[Pump Room]]
- [[Laboratory]]
- [[Security]]
- [[Locker Room]]
- [[Laundry Room]]
- [[Secret Passage]]

You are more likely to draft rooms with ducts if the door is currently powered. [^1]

Steam power is useful in [[Laboratory]], [[Garage]], [[Laundry Room]], [[Furnace]] and [[Pump Room]].

[^1]: [[Drafting Strategy - Architectural Digest Vol. 2]]
