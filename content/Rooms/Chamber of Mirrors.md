Upon drafting this room, there is only 1 exit available and you can access only one button. If you draft other rooms with doors around the other 3 sides, it changes the room to having up to 4 exits total. This also allows you to press the other buttons and rise or lower the glass partitions. This is important for accessing the upper part of the room: the center button is located on a rising platform that serves as an elevator. In total there are 8 item slots (4 in the lower part, 4 in the upper part).
