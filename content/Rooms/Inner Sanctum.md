On the ground there is the logo of [[The Estate|The Estate of Mount Holly]], text "[[Ovinn Nevarei]]" and 8 [[Sigil]] cores: diamond, bar with a ball, U-shape, chevron, hourglass, mountain, pentagram, rectangle and diamond interposed.

There are 8 doors, each representing one realm:
- [[Orinda Aries Sanctum Door]] ([[Orinda Aries]])
- [[Fenn Aries Sanctum Door]] ([[Fenn Aries]])
- [[Arch Aries Sanctum Door]] ([[Arch Aries]])
- [[Eraja Sanctum Door]] ([[Eraja]])
- [[Corarica Sanctum Door]] ([[Corarica]])
- Nuance Sanctum Door?
- Mora Jai Sanctum Door?
- ??? Sanctum Door

The doors can be permanently opened with [[Sanctum Key]] (one door each).
Behind the doors there is a puzzle to figure out the correct [[Sigil]] for each core, which gives access to postcards from Herbert's trips and a Mora Jai box.
