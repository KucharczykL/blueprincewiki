Historically speaking, sigils are not a new
tradition. They have been found in every
era and every culture and predate both written text and heraldic arts. However,
today when we use the term "sigil" we are referring specifically to Realm Sigils, those
crude circular marks of designation found on royal artifacts, buildings and older documents.

This book was written to make simple the understanding of these fascinating glyphs
and to clearly display the correlation between each sigil and the iconography of
that realm's royal art. "How is the image of
a leafless burning tree seen in geometric lines and what does that tell us of a
Kingdom's history?" Each line, each circle, and each variation tells a story of the land.
Even the adorned borders around the sigils
have special meaning.

[PICTURE OF VERTICAL WAVE]
Wavy lines are used to indicate extreme
HEAT. The scorched air and blazing sun
cause the otherwise straight rays to appear shimmering.

[PICTURE OF THREE VERTICAL PERPENDICULAR LINES]
A RAINY realm is indicated with rays
of three vertical lines *pouring* down.

[PICTURE OF ZIGZAGGED LINE]
For more extreme STORMY weather,
a jagged lighting shaped ray is used.

[PICTURE OF VERTICAL LINE WITH DOTS ON IT]
A ray dotted with falling circles is used
for colder realms in which SNOW is a
common occurence.

[PICTURE OF ARC]
WINDY weather is displayed on sigils
by the use of bent curved lines, rays
buckling under the high winds.

3. MOTES
[PICTURE OF 8 SYMBOLS: ASTERISK, THREE VERTICAL LINES ARRANGED HORIZONTALLY, THREE DOTS IN ELONGATED CIRCLE, SQUARE WITH DOT, DOUBLE TRIANGLE, BUILDING, SIGIL, FLOWER]

On each sigil, floating between the rays
are small figures called motes. From the
shape and style of the motes used on a
sigil we can tell the type of society a given
realm proclaimed, if they considered
themselves mighty in arms or blessed by artistic and economic fortune.

[PICTURE OF ASTERISK] Tribal - A primitive realm. Hunter and gatherers.
[PICTURE OF THREE DOTS IN ELONGATED CIRCLE] Agricultural - Harvest and farming based society and culture.
[PICTURE OF TWO TRIANGLES] Martial - Militari\[MISSING\]
in generation spanning\[MISSING\]

[PICTURE OF BUILDING] Metropolitan - Large city-state with a dense, urban and populous center.
[PICTURE OF THREE VERTICAL LINES ARRANGED HORIZONTALLY] Academic - A center for knowledge, advanced in various scholarly pursuits. These realms are home to many schools, universities and libraries.
[PICTURE OF SQURE WITH DOT] Industrial - Engineering driven culture with a focus on manufacturing, invention and production.
[PICTURE OF SIGIL] Spiritual - An elevated reverence for nature, animals and the forces that guide us.
[PICTURE OF FLOWER] Poetic - A strong focus on the advancement of the arts, writing, music, and language.

4. BORDER

Around each sigil is an adorned edge
serving as a container for the information
within. There are eight different borders,
each one corresponding to a different color.
These borders always correspond to the
banner and national colors of that specific
realm.
