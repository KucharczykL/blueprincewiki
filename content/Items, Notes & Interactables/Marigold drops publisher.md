Local author and popular personality, [[Marion Marigold]] has announced a split with her long term publishing partner, [[Ruffington Press]], citing personal reasons. The announcement came after the arrest last week of fellow author and colleague, [[Caleb Manning]], who Marigold has past referred to as her mentor, friend and confidant. Marigold is the third prominent writer to distance themselves from the Publisher this week.
